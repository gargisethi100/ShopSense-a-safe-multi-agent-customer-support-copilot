# ShopSense Warranty Policy

## [WAR-1] What the warranty covers and for how long

All electronics purchased from ShopSense carry a 12-month limited warranty
counted from the delivery date. All non-electronic products carry a
90-day quality guarantee counted from the delivery date. The warranty
covers manufacturing defects: faults in materials or workmanship that
appear during normal use as described in the product manual.

## [WAR-2] What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover:

- Accidental damage, including drops, spills, and cracked screens
- Damage from misuse, modification, or repair attempts by anyone other
  than ShopSense
- Normal wear such as scratches, fading, or battery capacity decline
- Products with a removed or altered serial number
- Items bought second-hand; the warranty applies to the original
  purchaser only and is not transferable

## [WAR-3] How to make a warranty claim

A warranty claim requires the order id as proof of purchase. The customer
should describe the fault and, where possible, provide photos or video.
Claims are reviewed within 2 business days. ShopSense may ask the
customer to run simple checks (for example, trying a different cable or
charger) before approving a claim.

## [WAR-4] Warranty remedies

For an approved warranty claim, ShopSense chooses the remedy in this
order: first repair, then replacement with the same or an equivalent
product if repair is not practical, then a refund if neither repair nor
replacement is possible. Warranty refunds follow the same approval and
payment process as regular refunds [RET-5]. Shipping costs for approved
warranty claims are always paid by ShopSense.
