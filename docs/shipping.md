# ShopSense Shipping Policy

## [SHP-1] Shipping methods and delivery times

ShopSense offers two shipping methods. Standard shipping takes 5 to 7
business days from the ship date. Express shipping takes 2 to 3 business
days from the ship date. Delivery times are counted from when the order
ships, not from when it is placed; order preparation before shipping
takes 1 to 2 business days.

## [SHP-2] Shipping costs

Standard shipping costs $4.99 and is free for orders of $50.00 or more,
calculated on the order total before taxes. Express shipping costs $12.99
on all orders regardless of order total. Shipping fees are refunded only
when the return is due to a damaged, defective, or wrong item as described
in the returns policy [RET-3].

## [SHP-3] Tracking

A tracking number is assigned when the order ships and is available in
the order details from that moment on. Orders in "processing" status do
not have a tracking number yet. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours
to appear after the carrier's first scan.

## [SHP-4] Late, lost, or stuck shipments

If a shipment shows no tracking movement for 7 consecutive days, or has
not arrived within 10 business days of shipping, the customer may request
an investigation with the carrier. If the carrier confirms the parcel is
lost, ShopSense sends a free replacement, or issues a full refund if the
customer prefers. Investigations typically take 3 to 5 business days.

## [SHP-5] Address changes

The delivery address can be changed only while the order status is
"processing". Once the order has shipped, the address can no longer be
changed by ShopSense; the customer may be able to redirect the parcel
directly with the carrier using their tracking number.
