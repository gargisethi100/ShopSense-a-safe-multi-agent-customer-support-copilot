# ShopSense Returns & Refunds Policy

## [RET-1] Return window

Items may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date shown in the
order's tracking record. The window is counted from the day the carrier
marked the order delivered, not from the day the order was placed. Orders
returned after the 30-day window are not eligible for a refund.

## [RET-2] What can and cannot be returned

Most items can be returned within the [RET-1] window. The following are
not eligible for return under any circumstances:

- Items marked "final sale" on the product page
- Gift cards
- Items that have been visibly used, washed, or damaged by the customer

Unopened items in original packaging are always eligible within the window.

## [RET-3] Damaged, defective, or wrong items

If an order arrives damaged, defective, or containing the wrong item, the
customer is entitled to a full refund of the amount paid for that order,
including any shipping fees. The customer does not need to ship the item
back for orders under $100; for orders of $100 or more, a prepaid return
label is provided. Damage claims must be reported within 14 days of
delivery.

## [RET-4] Change-of-mind returns

For undamaged items the customer simply no longer wants, the customer must
ship the item back in its original packaging. The refund is issued after
the returned item passes inspection at the warehouse, which takes 3 to 5
business days from arrival. Return shipping for change-of-mind returns is
paid by the customer.

## [RET-5] How and when refunds are paid

All refunds are reviewed and approved by a ShopSense team member before
money moves. Once approved, refunds are issued to the original payment
method and typically arrive within 5 to 10 business days. ShopSense does
not offer refunds to a different card or account than the one used for
the purchase.

## [RET-6] Order cancellations

An order can be cancelled only while its status is "processing". Once an
order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled - the customer should
instead wait for delivery and use the return process in [RET-1] and
[RET-4]. Cancelled orders are never charged; if a charge appears for a
cancelled order it should be reported to support.
